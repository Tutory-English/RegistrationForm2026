📄 Refund Policy Agreement
Please read the policy carefully before confirming your registration.
  1. Payments that have been completed cannot be refunded if the participant cancels the class.
  2. If cancellation happens due to participant error (for example: wrong schedule selection), the refund amount will be 80% of the total payment.
